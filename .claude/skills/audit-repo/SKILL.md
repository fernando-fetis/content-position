# Audit

The goal is a repository that is 100% in order and as clean as possible, as in its final version. The main session coordinates; subagents review one area each, in parallel; the main session verifies, runs the heavy checks and writes the report. Subagents cannot launch subagents of their own, so the split happens here.

## 1. Prepare (main session)
- Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md`, every skill in `.claude/skills/` (they hold the rules audited against), `README.md`, `Makefile` and `todo.md`; list the experiments (`experiments/*/`), the documents (`docs/*.typ`) and the state of the cluster (read-only `squeue`), since queued jobs restrict what may change in `src/`.
- Agree on the scope with the user if it is not the whole repository.

## 2. Review in parallel (subagents)
Launch the subagents in one message, each with a precise brief: its area and files, the skills to read, the checks below, that it may fix only errors it is sure about and only in the files of its area (areas never share files), that it must not commit, run real compute, rebuild summaries or touch the cluster, and that it reports every fix (file, what, why) and every open issue with a recommendation. Models: Sonnet for mechanical checks, Opus for anything that needs judgement; never Fable unless the user asks for it explicitly.
- **One per experiment** (Opus): the `audit-experiment` skill in full (completeness; every quantity against the definitions and methodology named in the README's Sources, both directions; every summary key used by the documents or a figure, and every reported number computed in `summarize`; every reported number and figure; conformity with the `experiment`, `figures` and `writing` skills).
- **Shared code** `src/` (Opus): every module works for every experiment that imports it, no deprecated APIs, docstrings accurate; nothing that pending cluster jobs read changes what it computes (docstring-only edits are safe).
- **The document** of `docs/`, one subagent per group of top-level sections (Opus; the theory, its validation, the applications and prior work can be split among two to four), against the `writing` and `figures` skills: every proof checked step by step, every statement exact under its hypotheses, nothing asserted that was not proved or measured; definitions before use, one meaning per symbol, no ambiguity; figures and captions consistent with the text, and every conclusion drawn from a figure consistent with its shape (a monotone claim against a U-shaped curve, a claim about all models against one that deviates); references cited and correct, and every work the text names or uses (datasets, benchmarks, models, methods) cited. Fix only errors of fact (a broken reference, a typo, a step with an evident correction); anything that changes what a statement says, its framing or the narrative is a proposal for the report, since the user edits the documents with coauthors.
- **Numbers of the document** (Sonnet): every number of the results texts (the document of `docs/`, and the notes of `.temp/` an experiment's Sources name) against its `results/summary.json`, model by model, with the rounding shown, and every conclusion against the numbers it cites; it reports mismatches and fixes none (the document and experiment subagents own those files). It can be skipped for experiments whose own audit already checked their numbers. Results of an experiment still running may be partial: a pending case is listed, not counted as an error, as long as the text says it is pending.
- **Repository files and configuration** (Sonnet): `Makefile` (targets and `make help`), `README.md` (accurate, and it never details the contents of `docs/`, `experiments/` or `.temp/`), `.gitignore` (raw results, builds, caches, `.env` and `.temp/` ignored; summaries and figures tracked), `requirements.txt` (pins what is imported), `data/` (only inputs that are used), `todo.md` (nothing obsolete, done or contradicted; items that can be done now), `.claude/` (consistent with the repository, generic: no document file names, no list or count of experiments).
- **Sensitive information and cleanliness** (Sonnet), over every tracked file (code, configs, summaries and their metadata, text inside figures, documents, READMEs, `.claude/`), never the git history: no personal or private information about anyone (names other than the authors of cited work, user names, e-mail addresses, personal host names, local paths such as `/Users/...` or `/home/...`, account or profile names, tokens, keys, secrets); cluster hosts and paths only where the setup needs them, with placeholders for each person's user; no traces of the private workflow (notes to someone, internal plans); nothing stale or superfluous (leftover files, backups, commented-out code, TODOs in code, unused data, empty folders, tracked build products or caches). It reports; the owner of each file fixes, or the main session does.

## 3. Verify (main session, one run at a time, in the background)
The scripts in `scripts/` (run from the repository root) do the mechanical checks the same way every time; launch the first two in the background, they take minutes.
- `.claude/skills/audit-repo/scripts/smoke.sh [<NN_name> ...]`: the smoke stages of every experiment (tiny-llama on the CPU), one line per experiment; real compute never runs locally.
- `.venv/bin/python .claude/skills/audit-repo/scripts/reproduce.py <NN_name> ...`: rebuilds each summary from the existing raw results, compares every key and array with the current one, reruns the figures and lists the PDFs that changed, then puts the summary and figures back as they were (it never changes them; to keep a rebuilt summary, `make summarize E=<NN_name>` and `.venv/bin/python src/run.py <NN_name> figures`).
- `.venv/bin/python .claude/skills/audit-repo/scripts/labels.py`: every label cited in code, configs and READMEs exists in a `.typ` file of `docs/` or `.temp/`, no label is defined twice in `docs/`, and no statement is cited by number.
- `.venv/bin/python .claude/skills/audit-repo/scripts/sensitive.py`: personal or private information in tracked files (local paths, e-mails, tokens, and the identity of whoever runs it, read at run time); its output goes to the sensitive-information subagent.
- `.claude/skills/figures/scripts/review.sh`: renders every figure for a visual check and fails on raster images.
- `make docs`: everything compiles, nothing overflows, no figure lands inside a proof.
- Review every change the subagents made (`git diff`), undo any that is not clearly right, and rerun the checks that cover the fixes. Verify every "unused" claim before deleting anything (search the documents for its numbers), and every new bibliography entry on its arXiv or venue page.

## 4. Report
Write `.temp/audit-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` (in English, concise): the scope; what was checked; every fix (file, what, why); the open issues with recommendations, by importance; the proposals for the documents; and the results of the verification runs. Do not commit: present the changes and the report to the user.
